How these counters work

A PDF response served is counted after the protected PDF generator finishes rendering and the signed-in recipient is authorized. This does not establish that the user's browser saved the file. A valid verification request records a public certificate lookup that found a valid certificate; repeated requests close together may be combined. The certificate's QR links to the existing public verification page. These metrics are not unique QR scans or unique people.
