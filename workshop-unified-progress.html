How certificate readiness is calculated

A student is marked ready for certificate review only if their workshop enrollment is founder-approved, the required number of attendance days are verified, and their submitted project has an approved mentor review. This is not automatic certificate issuance. Actual issued and revoked certificate records are shown separately.

Attendance management · Project reviews · Payment reconciliation

Records are private and visible only to authorized founder accounts. Large data sets are limited to 500 recent workshop registrations; no fake registrations, payments or certificates are generated.
