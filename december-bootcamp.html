Seven days of hands-on learning

  1. Introduction to AI, coding and technology

    Explore where these tools show up in daily life and try a beginner activity.

  2. Programming fundamentals and logical thinking

    Break problems into small steps and practise sequencing and decisions.

  3. HTML, CSS and website development

    Build and style a simple web page.

  4. Artificial intelligence and prompt engineering

    Write clearer prompts and compare the answers you get.

  5. Practical technology activities

    Use what you’ve learned to develop a small creative concept.

  6. Guided mini project

    Build and refine your project with guidance.

  7. Project presentation and feedback

    Show your work, explain your choices and receive feedback.
