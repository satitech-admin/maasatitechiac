Common questions

I'm a complete beginner. Can I start?

Yes. Open the course roadmap and start with the first skill. Follow the short learning guide and practice task before taking a quiz.

Where can I see my scores and completed skills?

Sign in to your student account and open My Learning or My Progress. Your verified activity appears in your personal dashboard.

How do I get my skill badge or certificate?

Complete the required practice, submit your project, pass the skill assessment and wait for instructor verification where required. Only approved credentials can be verified.

Do I have to pay on this page?

No. This page is a guide, not a payment form. Check the program page for its current fees and enrollment instructions.

Need help finding the right page?

Choose your goal above, follow the numbered steps, or open the student portal to see course and account options. You can return to this guide any time.
