Student registration privacy

What the registration form collects

The official Google Form asks for student and school information, a guardian contact number and permission, and payment details including a screenshot. Google requires sign-in for file uploads and explains what account information it records on the form.

How the session team uses it

The team uses the registration details to check eligibility and guardian permission, match the ₹199 payment, and contact you about your registration and the session. Staff record reviews in the private registration workbook. Use the official form to upload payment proof and keep unrelated account information out of the screenshot.

Registration and payment updates

Students under 18 should register with a parent or guardian. The ₹199 fee is non-refundable once registration is submitted. The session team sends the verification update by email or SMS.

Questions or corrections

Contact Maa Sati IAC if you need help correcting a submission or have a question about your registration information.
