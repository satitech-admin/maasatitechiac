Frequently asked questions

Are fees charged monthly or paid all at once?

The amounts shown are monthly training fees. Automatic recurring billing is not active. Do not assume a single payment covers an entire twelve-month course.

What happens after I apply?

The institute reviews actual enrollment or internship applications. Account verification and any applicable guardian consent must be completed before class access.

Will I receive a certificate automatically?

No. A valid certificate requires the institute's eligibility checks, completed coursework or approved projects, and a real issued verification record.

Where can I see interview practice and mock tests?

Open the twelve-month programs page for roadmaps, mock quizzes and interview questions, and sign in to store progress.
